## My Favorite Series: Shameless

**Genre:** Comedy-Drama
*Years aired:* 2011 - 2021

### About the Series
Shameless follows the Gallagher family, a chaotic household on the South Side of Chicago. The kids look after each other while their father, Frank, causes trouble.

### Why I Like It
- Funny and emotional at the same time
- Realistic story about family and struggle
- Strong, memorable characters
